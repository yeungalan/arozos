Apple Notes Sync
Bidirectional sync between arozos Notes and iPhone Notes via IMAP

Server Settings

Standard IMAP port is 143 (requires root) or 993 (IMAPS). Default 1143 avoids root requirement on most systems.
How It Works

ArozOS runs a minimal IMAP mail server that maps your Notes to email messages. Your iPhone's Notes app connects to this server using your arozos username and a dedicated access token (shown below). No email is actually sent anywhere.

Your IMAP Access Token

Use this token as the password when adding the IMAP account on your iPhone. Your username stays the same as your arozos login.

iPhone Setup Instructions

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Mail → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Mail Account.
  3. Fill in:
NameArozOS Notes
Emailyour-username@arozos
Password(your token from above)
DescriptionArozOS Notes
  1. Tap Next. When asked for incoming/outgoing server details:
Host Nameyour-arozos-host
Port1143
Usernameyour-username
Password(same token)
Outgoing (SMTP)Leave blank or tap None
  1. Tap Save. Disable Mail for this account but keep Notes enabled.
  2. Open the Notes app — your arozos notes will appear under this account.
Note on SSL: If your arozos instance does not have TLS, you may need to disable SSL for the incoming mail server in iPhone > Settings > Mail > Accounts > ArozOS Notes > Account > Advanced > Use SSL → Off, and set Authentication to Password.
