IMAP Server for Apple Notes

arozos acts as an IMAP server. Add it as a Mail account on your iPhone or Mac, then enable Notes for that account — your arozos Notes will appear in Apple Notes.

How to connect Apple Notes
  1. 1 On iPhone/iPad: Settings → Mail → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Mail Account.
    On Mac: System Settings → Internet Accounts → Add Account → Other Mail Account.
  2. 2 Enter any name, your arozos username as the email (e.g. admin@arozos.local), and your arozos password.
  3. 3 Set IMAP. Incoming mail server: your-arozos-ip port 1143. Username = arozos username, Password = arozos password.
  4. 4 Outgoing (SMTP) is not used — enter any placeholder host and disable SMTP or skip it if possible.
  5. 5 After the account is added, go to Settings → Notes → Accounts and enable Notes for the new account.
  6. 6 Open the Notes app. Tap the back arrow to see account folders. Your arozos Notes appear under the new account's Notes folder.
